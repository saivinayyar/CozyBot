Welcome to CozyBot

This is an AI chatbot made to help children and young adults by giving them a place to vent and talk about their feelings to a no-judgement source available at all times, especially at times when there is no one else to talk to.

A few things to know

CozyBot does not request personal details such as your name, address, or school, and each conversation is not retained after the page is closed.

It does not take sides between parents, as that is not its purpose.

If a message suggests a serious safety concern, CozyBot will pause its usual tone and direct you toward a trusted adult or a helpline instead.

This is an independent project rather than an official application, so it may not be perfect.

If this feels like too much right now

If you are not safe or this is an emergency, please call 112.

If you need someone to talk to, Childline India can be reached at 1098. It is free, available at all hours, and intended specifically for children and teenagers.

You may also speak with someone you trust, such as a parent, teacher, or older sibling. Asking for help is always acceptable, even when it feels difficult.

CozyBot is not a replacement for the people who care about you. It is intended to provide support during moments when they are not available.
